Hamburger

Classic American Hamburger

A foundational comfort food and the undisputed centerpiece of backyard cookouts and diners worldwide, this classic cheeseburger is simple yet deeply satisfying. Its roots trace back to late 19th-century German immigrants in the United States who adapted seasoned Hamburg-style minced beef steaks to fit between slices of bread for quick, portable meals. The secret to elevating a homemade burger from ordinary to unforgettable lies entirely in the quality of the beef—specifically an 80/20 lean-to-fat ratio that guarantees juiciness—combined with high-heat searing to achieve the perfect caramelized crust while keeping the interior tender.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Hamburger buns
  • Cheddar cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Red Onion
  • Salt
  • Pipper

Steps

  1. Shape the ground beef into even patties with a shallow center dimple and season both sides with salt and pepper.
  2. Preheat a cast-iron skillet or grill over high heat until smoking hot.
  3. Sear the patties for 3 to 4 minutes per side and top with cheddar cheese during the last minute to melt.
  4. Toast the buttered hamburger buns lightly until golden brown.
  5. Assemble the burger with lettuce, tomato, red onion, and the melted-cheese patty.
